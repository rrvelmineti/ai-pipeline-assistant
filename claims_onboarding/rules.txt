# Claims onboarding rules for the platform's target fields.
#
# One field per line:   field name | what the field means | rules separated by commas
#
# Rules you can use (not case sensitive):
#   required                       the field must be mapped and every row must have a value
#   unique                         no two rows may share the same value
#   number                         a plain number such as 125.50 (no $ signs or commas)
#   whole number                   digits only
#   not negative                   zero or more
#   exactly N digits               for example: exactly 10 digits
#   exactly N characters           for example: exactly 5 characters
#   date YYYY-MM-DD                also DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY, MM-DD-YYYY, YYYY/MM/DD
#   not in the future              a date that is not after today
#   not before OTHER_FIELD         a date that is not earlier than another date field
#   not greater than OTHER_FIELD   a number that is not larger than another number field
#   ICD-10 format                  looks like an ICD-10-CM diagnosis code, such as E11.9
#   one of A / B / C               one of the listed values, separated by slashes
#
# Add (warning) after a rule to report it without blocking go-live.
# Everything else is a blocker: the file is not ready until it is fixed.

claim_id         | Unique number identifying the claim                  | required, unique
member_id        | Insured member's identifier                          | required
member_dob       | Member's date of birth                               | required, date YYYY-MM-DD, not in the future
provider_npi     | Rendering provider's National Provider Identifier    | required, exactly 10 digits
service_date     | Date the service was provided                        | required, date YYYY-MM-DD, not in the future, not before member_dob
diagnosis_code   | Primary ICD-10-CM diagnosis code                     | required, ICD-10 format
procedure_code   | CPT or HCPCS procedure code                          | required, exactly 5 characters
place_of_service | Two-digit CMS place of service code                  | exactly 2 digits (warning)
billed_amount    | Total amount charged by the provider                 | required, number, not negative
allowed_amount   | Amount allowed under the provider contract           | number, not negative, not greater than billed_amount (warning)
paid_amount      | Amount paid by the health plan                       | required, number, not negative, not greater than billed_amount
claim_status     | Adjudication status of the claim                     | required, one of paid / denied / pending / adjusted
